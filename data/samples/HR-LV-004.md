# HR-LV-004 | Leave and Attendance for Project Staff
Owner: HR Business Partner | Version: v2.1 | Updated: Apr 2026

## Applying for leave
Apply for leave in the HR portal and get approval from your reporting manager. Planned leave of more than 3 days must be applied for at least 2 weeks in advance so that client cover can be arranged.

## Client-facing roles
Staff on client-facing or on-call rosters must inform the delivery lead and arrange a named backup before leave is approved. Update the shared team calendar and the client's roster sheet.

## Attendance
Mark attendance daily in the HR portal. Work-from-home days need manager approval and are limited to the number agreed in the client contract. Regularise missed punches within 3 working days.
