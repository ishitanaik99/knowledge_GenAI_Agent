# POL-KYC-003 | Periodic KYC Update (Re-KYC)
Owner: Compliance | Version: v5.0 | Updated: Jun 2026

## Re-KYC frequency
Periodic KYC update is due at least once every 2 years for high-risk customers, once every 8 years for medium-risk customers and once every 10 years for low-risk customers, counted from the date of account opening or the last KYC update.

## Self-declaration route
If there is no change in KYC information, the customer may submit a self-declaration through net banking, mobile banking, email from the registered address, a letter or an ATM. If only the address has changed, the customer submits a self-declaration of the new address, which is verified within two months.

## Overdue accounts
Accounts with re-KYC overdue receive three reminders, at least one by letter. Before any restriction is applied, the customer must be given notice. Operations must not freeze an account for overdue KYC without written approval from the Compliance desk.
