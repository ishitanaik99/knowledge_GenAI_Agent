# SOP-INC-011 | Incident Severity and Escalation
Owner: Service Management | Version: v3.0 | Updated: Aug 2026

## Severity levels
Severity 1: a critical service such as UPI, net banking or ATM switch is down for all users. Severity 2: a critical service is degraded or down for a subset of users. Severity 3: a non-critical function is affected and a workaround exists. Severity 4: cosmetic issues and service requests.

## Response and update times
Severity 1: acknowledge within 15 minutes, notify the client duty manager within 30 minutes and send updates every 30 minutes. Severity 2: acknowledge within 30 minutes with hourly updates. Severity 3: acknowledge within 4 business hours.

## Root cause analysis
A root cause analysis (RCA) report is due within 5 business days of resolving any Severity 1 or Severity 2 incident. The RCA covers timeline, root cause, customer impact, corrective actions and preventive actions with owners and dates.
