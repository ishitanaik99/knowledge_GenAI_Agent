# SOP-DLV-021 | Production Change Request
Owner: Delivery Governance | Version: v1.8 | Updated: Sep 2026

## Standard change
Any change to the client's production environment needs an approved change request (CR) in the ITSM tool. Raise a standard CR at least 5 business days before the planned release, with impact analysis, test evidence from UAT, rollback plan and the deployment window.

## Approval
Standard CRs are approved by the Change Advisory Board (CAB), which meets every Tuesday and Thursday. The CR needs sign-off from the project manager and the client application owner before it is placed on the CAB agenda.

## Emergency change
An emergency change is allowed only to restore service during a Severity 1 or Severity 2 incident. It needs verbal approval from the client duty manager, followed by a retrospective CR within 24 hours.

## Release windows
Production releases run in the approved window of Saturday 11 PM to Sunday 5 AM IST. Month-end (last 3 working days) and quarter-end are change freeze periods unless the CAB approves an exception.
