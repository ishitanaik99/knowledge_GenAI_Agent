# SOP-DSP-009 | Failed Transaction Disputes (ATM and UPI)
Owner: Disputes Desk | Version: v4.1 | Updated: Jul 2026

## ATM cash not dispensed
When an account is debited but cash is not dispensed at an ATM, the amount must be auto-reversed within T+5 calendar days, where T is the transaction date. If the reversal is late, the bank pays the customer compensation of Rs 100 per day of delay, credited without the customer having to claim it.

## UPI debit without credit
If a UPI payment is debited from the customer but not credited to the beneficiary, the amount must be reversed within T+1 day. Delay beyond T+1 attracts compensation of Rs 100 per day. Raise the case in the UPI dispute portal with the 12-digit UPI reference number.

## Logging a dispute
Log every dispute in the complaint management system with category, transaction reference, amount and date. The system assigns a ticket number, which must be shared with the customer. Escalate cases older than 10 days to the Disputes Desk team lead.
