# SOP-OPS-014 | Customer Address Change
Owner: Retail Operations | Version: v3.2 | Updated: Aug 2026

## Scope
Applies to change of communication or permanent address for individual savings and current account customers. Address change for corporate accounts follows SOP-OPS-019 and is out of scope.

## Documents required
Customer submits a signed address change request with one Officially Valid Document (OVD) showing the new address: Aadhaar, passport, driving licence, voter ID or NREGA job card. If the OVD does not show the new address, a utility bill not older than two months is accepted as deemed proof, and the customer must submit an updated OVD within three months.

## Processing steps
1. Maker verifies the OVD against the original or a DigiLocker copy. 2. Maker updates the address in the core banking system (CBS) and scans the request. 3. Checker approves the change in CBS the same day. 4. System sends an SMS and email confirmation to the registered mobile and email. Maker and checker must be different employees.

## Turnaround time
Branch requests are completed within 2 working days. Requests through net banking with Aadhaar e-KYC are updated within 1 working day. Breaches of turnaround time are reported in the weekly operations MIS.
