# SOP-CARD-007 | Debit Card Block and Reissue
Owner: Cards Operations | Version: v2.4 | Updated: Sep 2026

## Blocking a lost or stolen card
Customers can block a lost or stolen debit card instantly through mobile banking, net banking, the 24x7 phone banking IVR or by SMS BLOCK followed by the last four digits of the card to the registered number. Branch staff can block a card in the card management system after verifying the customer with two security questions.

## Reissue
A replacement card is ordered automatically when the customer chooses reissue during blocking. The card is dispatched to the registered address within 7 working days. Reissue fee is Rs 200 plus GST, waived for premium accounts and for cards damaged on delivery.

## Disputed transactions after loss
If the customer reports unauthorised transactions on a lost card, raise a dispute under SOP-DSP-009 and note the time the card was blocked. Customer liability is zero for transactions reported within 3 working days of the customer receiving the alert.
